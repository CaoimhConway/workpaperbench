Celgene closed November 20, 2019 as a business combination. Cash consideration was 35,745. Other consideration comprised common stock 40,378, common-holder CVRs 1,644, replacement options 1,428, restricted awards 987 and award-holder CVRs 87. Reported total consideration was 80,269. Acquired cash was 11,179. The final allocation included goodwill 13,990 and intangible assets 63,927. Acquisition expenses were 657. CVRs represented conditional future payments, not closing cash. Post-combination service awards of approximately 1,000 were compensation outside acquisition consideration.

MyoKardia closed November 17, 2020. The transaction was an asset acquisition because substantially all gross asset value, excluding cash and deferred taxes, related to mavacamten. Cash paid for shares was 12,030 and for awards 1,059, totaling 13,089. The table deducts 482 for unvested awards and includes transaction costs 53 in allocation cost 12,660. ## b01:s08

Authored analytical scope

authored_assumption

Deal cash metrics cover only the listed cash consideration and acquired cash, with Otezla as the sole specified required divestiture. They exclude transaction fees, financing flows and taxes. The source does not establish cash payment timing for the capitalized MyoKardia transaction costs. Do not infer that accounting cost is a cash payment. The disclosed transaction-cost amounts retain their source accounting bases: Celgene acquisition expenses and MyoKardia costs included in asset allocation. Zero components in acquisitions are structurally absent from the listed consideration, not a claim that every other possible transaction cash flow was zero. For cash_coverage, retain the reported operating cash and investing-category totals. Do not attempt to allocate the remaining acquisition/other payments to individual undisclosed fees or other transactions. Equity and debt financing and marketable-security turnover are outside the requested cash remainder. The earnings diagnostic excludes only acquisition-date MyoKardia expenses and all contingent-consideration fair-value changes in 2020. Keep other income-statement items unchanged and make no tax adjustment. It is not issuer non-GAAP earnings or a fully normalized continuing-business result. The revenue comparison uses actual 2020 revenue and the supplied 2019 Celgene/Otezla pro forma baseline. It does not assert constant currency, organic growth or a forecast. Source pro forma qualifications remain applicable.
