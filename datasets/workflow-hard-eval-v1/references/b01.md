## Acquisition bases

Celgene consideration includes issued stock, conditional rights and replacement
awards. Their acquisition-date values are accounting consideration, not cash
paid at closing. The acquisition expense is outside business-combination
consideration.

- Noncash consideration is `40,378 + 1,644 + 1,428 + 987 + 87 = 44,524`.
- Accounting consideration is `35,745 + 44,524 = 80,269`.
- Listed closing cash net of acquired cash is `35,745 - 11,179 = 24,566`.
- After the specified required disposal, that cash amount is
  `24,566 - 13,400 = 11,166`.

The MyoKardia cash-award amount contains both acquisition consideration and
compensation. The unvested service component is compensation. The remaining
`1,059 - 482 = 577` is cash consideration for awards in the acquisition basis.

- Accounting allocation cost is `12,030 + 577 + 53 = 12,660`.
- Listed cash net of acquired cash is `12,030 + 1,059 - 861 = 12,228`.
- The noncompensation portion is `12,030 + 577 - 861 = 11,746`.
- No additional required disposal is included for MyoKardia in the stated scope.

Both reconstructed bases equal the independently reported totals in the original
input. A changed reported total must produce a nonzero residual, defined as
reconstructed less reported.

The 53 of capitalized transaction costs affects accounting allocation. Its cash
payment date is not established. Therefore it is excluded from these defined
cash metrics. Neither the 657 Celgene acquisition expense nor the 53 MyoKardia
cost is treated as proof of a closing cash payment.

An independent balance-sheet route sums the signed acquired assets, liabilities
and goodwill. It yields 80,269 for Celgene. For MyoKardia it yields
`11,553 + 861 + 295 + 177 - 226 = 12,660`. The 11,553 intangible allocation also
ties to exact IPRD expense 11,438 plus licenses 115. This confirms that the
separately expensed research asset is already part of the asset-acquisition cost.

## Company cash coverage

The cash-flow statement already includes the 482 cash compensation within
operating cash. Subtracting the entire 12,228 MyoKardia cash amount from that
operating cash would count the compensation twice. The portion to compare with
acquisition investing payments is 11,746.

| Year | Operating cash before listed deal compensation | Identified noncompensation acquisition cash | Other acquisition/other payments | Other divestiture/other proceeds | Cash after specified portfolio investment |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2019 | 8,210 | 24,566 | 211 | 2,452 | -1,551 |
| 2020 | 14,534 | 11,746 | 1,338 | 870 | 1,085 |

The remaining acquisition amounts are `24,777 - 24,566 = 211` and
`13,084 - 11,746 = 1,338`. These are unallocated remainders in the disclosed
investing category. They are not assumed fees, errors or undocumented cash
payments. Other 2019 disposal proceeds are `15,852 - 13,400 = 2,452`.

The statutory-category route gives:

- 2019: `8,210 - 836 - 24,777 + 15,852 = -1,551`.
- 2020: `14,052 - 753 - 13,084 + 870 = 1,085`.

A separate cash ledger first removes the 482 compensation use from operating
cash, then deducts gross deal cash and the remaining investing payments:
`14,534 - 753 - 12,228 - 1,338 + 870 = 1,085`.
This equivalence depends on the disclosed operating classification, not merely
on applying the same formula twice.

The remainder excludes securities turnover and financing. It retains the taxes
and other cash uses already included in reported operating cash. It is not the
change in total cash and is not an assertion that acquisitions caused the
change between years.

## Performance views

The statutory 2019 revenue base includes only Celgene's post-acquisition period.
The disclosed 2019 pro forma revenue instead applies the specified
Celgene/Otezla perimeter for the year. Thus:

- Actual-base growth: `(42,518 / 26,145 - 1) × 100 = 62.623828647925%`.
- Pro forma-baseline growth: `(42,518 / 39,759 - 1) × 100 = 6.939309338766%`.

Neither number identifies organic growth. The comparison preserves the source's
pro forma qualifications and does not claim a fully constant business perimeter
or constant currency.

MyoKardia's acquisition-date expense is `11,438 + 241 + 241 = 11,920`.
The specified pretax diagnostic is `-6,871 + 11,920 - 1,757 = 3,292`. Removing a
contingent-consideration gain reduces earnings. The 1,757 is the entire disclosed
fair-value effect, not solely the Celgene CVR component.

A separate income-statement reconstruction excludes the selected charges within
their original expense lines:

`42,518 - 11,773 - (7,661 - 241) - (11,143 - 241) - 9,688 - (-2,314 + 1,757) = 3,292`.

This retains other acquisition-related amortization, impairments, financing and
operating effects. It is intentionally narrower than issuer adjusted earnings.
No tax benefit or continuing-business earnings growth is inferred.

## Claim adjudication

1. **Contradicted.** MyoKardia allocation cost 12,660 differs from net listed cash
   12,228. Note 3 alone is sufficient.
2. **Supported.** Otezla generated 13,400 cash, with no reported pretax disposal
   gain. The specific divestiture disclosure is sufficient.
3. **Unresolved.** The source identifies the specified pro forma comparison,
   not the actual organic-growth quantity asserted by the claim.

## Alternatives, controls and limitations

The alternative SQL establishes acquisition bases from signed asset allocations,
rebuilds cash coverage using compensation-neutral operating cash and gross deal
payments, and reconstructs the pretax diagnostic within expense categories.
Equivalent SQL and row order are accepted. Additional relevant cash-flow and
allocation context is allowed without requiring every contextual citation.

Ten controls exercise compensation classification, acquired cash, unpaid
transaction costs, noncash share valuation, contingent remeasurement, a reported
basis discrepancy, pre-acquisition revenue, current revenue, monetary scaling
and row order. Dependent observations move coherently. For example, moving 118
of unchanged MyoKardia cash into compensation reduces investing cash and IPRD
cost, increases operating compensation, and leaves total cash and immediate
expense unchanged. An accrued fee changes accounting cost and earnings without
changing cash. The reported-total discrepancy is intentional and must remain
visible.

Targeted wrong submissions confuse book cost and cash, treat all award payments
as compensation, assume fees were paid, count compensation twice, treat the
expensed research asset as an additional operating cash outflow, use the
statutory comparator as pro forma, omit the award expenses, or reverse the
fair-value adjustment sign.

The focused tests check both SQL paths on original and control inputs, acquired
net-asset identities, direct expense-line reconstruction and cash classification.
Native materialization and isolated grading are separate integration gates.
Optional explanatory prose is not semantically graded. Independent human review,
external-user validation and empirical difficulty evidence remain pending.
