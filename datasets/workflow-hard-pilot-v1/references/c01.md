Products in each geography equal its sales total less Services. Their sum then automatically equals 294866. These nonnegative joint allocations preserve both disclosed marginals. Boundary zeroes are allowed by this mathematical uncertainty set, not claims about Apple's actual sales mix. No common regional product mix is assumed.

Each geography's Products bounds follow from the capacity of its complement. The intervals are U.S. [46027, 142196], China [0, 66952], and Other [85718, 181887]. They are individually sharp. Their endpoints cannot be selected independently and combined into an arbitrary joint allocation.

The scenario reduces Products/Services cell sales by 5%/2% in the U.S., 25%/10% in China and 12%/3% in Other. Lost sales carry worldwide business-line margins in every geography. The lost sales carry the cost-of-sales reduction implied by those margins. No further demand response, geographic switching or other profit effects apply. This defines a gross-profit sensitivity, not operating profit or cash flow.
