# Microsoft cash investment and PPE movements

The [FY2025 annual report](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar25/index.html) supplies the [mapped facts and extract hash](../sources/microsoft-2025.json). The economic year ends June 30, 2025. Opening balances are June 30, 2024. Outputs are USD million.

`annual_expenses.ppe_depreciation_billion` maps to b01:s02. Its other columns map to b01:s03. All `finance_leases` columns map to b01:s03. Capital purchase payables map to b01:s02, not the operating cash-flow accounts-payable adjustment.

Capital cash outlay is an authored metric, not an issuer-labelled subtotal. It is `64551 + 2283 = 66834`. The 1372 of finance-lease interest is excluded by the metric. The 20511 of finance ROU assets obtained for obligations is noncash recognition and is shown separately. Paying lease principal settles a liability and does not identify newly recognized ROU assets.

PPE purchase payables rise from 4.3 to 6.9 billion, so the displayed-value change is 2600 million. Cash plus that increase gives a purchase proxy of `64551 + 2600 = 67151`. The proxy would equal purchase additions only with additional assumptions about all noncash payable movements and matching scope. The task does not assert those assumptions as observed facts.

The total-PPE bridge treats Note 6 depreciation as covering its PPE population, including the finance-lease subset. That scope convention is explicit in the contract. Lease amortization is not subtracted a second time. The cash-flow adjustment of 34153 includes amortization and other items and is not the Note 6 PPE depreciation amount.

The 3713 residual is unallocated. It is not measured acquisitions, FX, disposals, extra spending or a reporting error. The three billion-denominated inputs are rounded to 0.1 billion. Arithmetic precision does not establish million-dollar economic precision. Under an additional nearest-0.1-billion rounding assumption, the purchase-proxy uncertainty can be 100 million and the net residual uncertainty 150 million. Those illustrative rounding intervals are not graded targets.

The materially different reference route works through gross and accumulated balances. Total gross movement is 86607 and accumulated movement is 17232. The respective residuals are `86607 - 67151 - 20511 = -1055` and `17232 - 22000 = -4768`. Their difference is `-1055 - (-4768) = 3713`. The same calculation gives the finance-lease net residual `1117 - 67 = 1050`. Tests check these balance identities independently of the net-balance oracle.

The claims are contradicted, supported and unresolved. Lease principal differs from noncash recognition. Net finance-lease assets grew by more than principal payments. No supplied complete movement schedule attributes the total residual to acquired PPE.

Controls test offsetting cash/payable timing, nested noncash lease recognition, principal settlement, an unrelated cash-flow adjustment, scaling and an unallocated gross/net asset movement. The latter preserves gross less accumulated equals net. Reference and alternate queries preserve these identities. Independent human source review remains pending.
