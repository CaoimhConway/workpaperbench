## c04:s04 - Prior-quarter service revenue timing

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316124000058/v-20240930.htm
Locator: Item 7, Key Business Drivers, footnote (1), page 43
Publication: 2024-11-13, SEC filing accepted 17:14:01 (index does not label timezone)

Service revenue in a given quarter is primarily assessed on nominal payments volume in the prior quarter. The filing states service revenue reported for the twelve months ended September 30, 2024, 2023 and 2022 was based on nominal payments volume reported by financial institution clients for the twelve months ended June 30, 2024, 2023 and 2022, respectively.

## c04:s05 - Nominal payment-volume definition and scope

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316124000058/v-20240930.htm
Locator: Item 7, Key Business Drivers, definitions and volume footnotes, pages 42-43
Publication: 2024-11-13, SEC filing accepted 17:14:01 (index does not label timezone)

Payments volume is the aggregate dollar amount of purchases made with Visa-branded cards and other form factors, and excludes Europe co-badged volume. Nominal payments volume is denominated in U.S. dollars using Visa-established exchange rates applied to local-currency volume reported by clients. Visa notes some tables may not recalculate exactly because of rounding.
