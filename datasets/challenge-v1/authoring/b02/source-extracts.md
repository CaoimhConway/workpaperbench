## Report definitions and dates - PDF page 2

- Reported USDC circulation is total supply on approved chains less Tokens Allowed But Not Issued and Access Denied Tokens.
- Fair value of assets held in the USDC Reserve is the total balance of USD-denominated assets in the Circle Reserve Fund and segregated accounts at regulated financial institutions on behalf of holders.
- The observations are March 19 and March 31, 2025, each at 11:59 p.m. UTC. The report lists total supply of 60,459,258,338 and 61,073,416,469 USDC, respectively. The listed exclusions are 1,280,267,228 and 96,298,507 on March 19, and 996,628,065 and 101,016,689 on March 31. The report lists circulation of 59,082,692,603 and 59,975,771,715, respectively.
