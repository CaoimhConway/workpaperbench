## c02:s04 - Coinbase Trading Volume definition and scope

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1679788/000167978825000022/coin-20241231.htm
Locator: Item 7, Key Business Metrics, Trading Volume
Publication: 2025-02-13, SEC filing accepted 16:16:43 (index does not label timezone)

Trading Volume is the U.S. dollar equivalent of spot matched trades between buyers and sellers through Coinbase during the measurement period. It excludes derivatives volume and trades executed on third-party venues. Derivatives transaction revenue is recorded in consumer and institutional transaction revenue.

## c02:s05 - Coinbase transaction revenue and spot-volume reporting note

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1679788/000167978825000021/q424shareholderletter.htm
Locator: Shareholder letter, business discussion following Trading Volume table, page 4
Publication: 2025-02-13, 8-K accepted 16:10:21 (index does not label timezone)

The letter states consumer and institutional derivatives volume and market share reached highs in Q4. It says their revenue is recorded in consumer and institutional transaction revenue, while reported Trading Volume includes only matched spot volume on Coinbase and excludes derivatives.

## c02:s06 - Coinbase net revenue categories and periods

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1679788/000167978825000022/coin-20241231.htm

The filing reports annual transaction revenue and subscription and services revenue as components of total net revenue. Total revenue is a broader line that also includes other revenue. The transaction and subscription/service categories shown for 2023 were reclassified to conform to the 2024 presentation.
