# Retained factual extracts

These are concise factual extracts from the issuer-hosted reports. Original report PDFs are not included. Each fact retains a stable page and row locator at the original source URL.

## May 2025 report

Source: https://brale.xyz/assets/reports/SBC-Stable-Coin-Reserve-Attestation-Report-05-2025.pdf

- Page 2, SBC Reserve Report table: SBC issued 7,093,795, cash and cash equivalents USD 2,210,784, U.S. government backed debt USD 4,883,011, reported fair value of reserve assets USD 7,093,795.
- Page 2, Criteria & Notes 1-3: issued amount is total SBC supply on supported blockchains at the report date. Reported fair value is the total balance of U.S.-denominated assets held in unencumbered accounts segregated from other Brale accounts. The listed supported chains are Arbitrum, Avalanche, Base, Canton, Celo, Coreum, Ethereum, Ethereum Classic, Hedera, Optimism, Polygon, Solana and Stellar.
- Page 1, report date and opinion: measurement is May 31, 2025 at 11:50 p.m. Eastern Time. The report is dated June 12, 2025. Abdo states that it examined management's assertion under AICPA attestation standards.

Source: https://brale.xyz/assets/reports/SBC-Stable-Coin-Reserve-Attestation-Report-06-2025.pdf

- Page 2, SBC Reserve Report table: SBC issued 9,356,516, cash and cash equivalents USD 3,475,633, U.S. government backed debt USD 5,880,883, reported fair value of reserve assets USD 9,356,516.
- Page 2, Criteria & Notes 1-3: issued amount is total SBC supply on supported blockchains at the report date. Reported fair value is the total balance of U.S.-denominated assets held in unencumbered accounts segregated from other Brale accounts. The supported-chain list matches the May report.
