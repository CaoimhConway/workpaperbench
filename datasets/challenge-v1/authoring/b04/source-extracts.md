Source: https://cdn.sanity.io/files/ior4a5y3/production/e5689456575be999b570160ec235451f3a77b221.pdf/Standard%20Custody%20and%20Trust%20LLC%20May%202025%20RLUSD%20Reserves%20Report%20-%20Issued.pdf

- PDF page 3, printed page 2: May 22 and May 30, 2025 at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time. Circulating RLUSD: May 22 310,543,072, May 30 309,043,794. Reported reserve values: May 22 USD 323,349,533, May 30 USD 322,099,766.
- Reserve components, USD: May 22 Treasury bills 181,273,927, government money-market funds 97,445,721, cash deposits 44,629,885. May 30 Treasury bills 181,364,719, government money-market funds 97,606,162, cash deposits 43,128,885.
- PDF page 4, printed page 3: RLUSD outstanding includes issued, outstanding, redeemable tokens present on XRPL and Ethereum. Reserve accounts are established for holder benefit and segregated from company proprietary assets. Market value is determined at trade date and fair value at report date.
