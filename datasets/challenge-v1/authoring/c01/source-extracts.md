## c01:s06 - PayPal key metric definitions

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391725000019/pypl-20241231.htm
Locator: Item7 Key Performance Metrics and Net Revenues

TPV is the value of payments, net of payment reversals, successfully completed on the platform or enabled by partner solutions, excluding gateway-exclusive transactions. Transaction revenues comprise net transaction fees and other fees such as currency conversion. Other value added services include partnerships, interest and credit fees, referrals, subscriptions and gateway services. Active accounts can include multiple accounts per user.

## c01:s07 - PayPal revenue drivers

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391725000019/pypl-20241231.htm
Locator: Item7 Net Revenue Analysis, FY2024 transaction revenues

The source reports that transaction revenue growth was lower than TPV growth in 2024 primarily due to changes in merchant mix, with higher volume from large merchants having lower pricing. Both metrics concern the consolidated platform and are annual flows. The aggregate ratio does not identify any individual contract rate.
