# Visa fiscal 2024 and fiscal 2023 disclosures

c04:s01 - Annual revenue categories (USD million)
FY2024, year ended September 30: service revenue 16,114, data processing revenue 17,714, international transaction revenue 12,665, other revenue 3,197, client incentives (13,764), net revenue 35,926.
FY2023: service revenue 14,826, data processing revenue 16,007, international transaction revenue 11,638, other revenue 2,479, client incentives (12,297), net revenue 32,653. Client incentives are negative in the net revenue bridge.

c04:s02 - Nominal payments volume (USD billion)
Visa total: 12,984 for the twelve months ended June 30, 2024 and 12,087 for the twelve months ended June 30, 2023. The source table separates U.S. and international volumes and reports Visa totals.

c04:s03 - Fiscal-year processed transactions
Visa-processed transactions were 233,758 million for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2024 and 212,579 million for FY2023. This is a separate activity measure with a different period window from the nominal payment-volume table.

c04:s04 - Service revenue timing
Service revenue for a quarter is primarily assessed using the prior quarter's nominal payments volume. Visa states that service revenue reported for the twelve months ended September 30, 2024, 2023 and 2022 was based on nominal client-reported payments volume for the twelve months ended June 30 of each year. The Q4 release separately identifies June-ended volume underlying Q4 service revenue and September-quarter volume.

c04:s05 - Scope of payment volume
Payments volume is an aggregate purchase-value measure for Visa-branded cards and other form factors and excludes Europe co-badged volume. Nominal volume converts client-reported local-currency volume using Visa-established U.S.-dollar exchange rates. It is a dollar amount, not a transaction count.

c04:s06 - Fiscal Q4 timing and activity
The FY2024 release says fiscal Q4 service revenue was USD 4.2 billion, up 8%, and was recognized based on prior-quarter payment volume. Payment volume for the three months ended June 30, 2024, which underlay fiscal Q4 service revenue, rose 7% in constant dollars. Volume for the three months ended September 30 rose 8%.

## Data dictionary
`period_end` refers to the stated flow window end, with `months` equal to twelve. Annual revenue rows end September 30 and use USD million. Payment volume rows end June 30 and use USD billion. Processed transaction rows end September 30 and use millions of transactions.
