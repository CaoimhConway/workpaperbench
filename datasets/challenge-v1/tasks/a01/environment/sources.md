## Data dictionary
Amounts, units and signed source adjustments retain the displayed source precision. months denotes the duration of the reporting period. period_end identifies actual economic periods. Future guidance periods keep fiscal labels where target tables supply no date. Section IDs locate source tables. No live data is required.
