# Coinbase FY2024 and FY2023 disclosures

c02:s01 - Annual transaction revenue (USD thousand)
2024: consumer net 3,430,322, institutional net 345,598, other transaction revenue net 210,193, total transaction revenue 3,986,113.
2023: consumer net 1,334,018, institutional net 90,164, other transaction revenue net 95,472, total transaction revenue 1,519,654. The filing reclassified 2023 revenue categories to conform to the 2024 presentation.

c02:s02 - Annual subscription and services and net revenue (USD thousand)
2024 subscription and services revenue 2,307,133 and total net revenue 6,293,246. 2023 subscription and services revenue 1,406,886 and total net revenue 2,926,540. Transaction revenue plus subscription and services revenue equals total net revenue in these rows. Other revenue is outside total net revenue.

c02:s03 - Annual Trading Volume by customer type (USD billion)
Consumer: 221 in 2024 and 75 in 2023. Institutional: 941 and 393. Total: 1,162 and 468. These values are rounded in the shareholder letter.

c02:s04 - Metric definition and revenue scope
Trading Volume is the U.S. dollar equivalent value of spot matched trades between a buyer and seller through Coinbase during the measurement period. The 10-K excludes derivatives volume and third-party venue trades from Trading Volume.

c02:s05 - Derivatives revenue reporting
Coinbase states consumer and institutional derivatives volume and market share reached highs in Q4. Revenue from these products is included in consumer and institutional transaction revenue, while reported Trading Volume excludes derivatives volume.

c02:s06 - Period and units
Both revenue values are fiscal-year flows for years ended December 31 and are reported in USD thousands. Trading Volume values are full-year flows in USD billions and rounded to whole billions.

## Data dictionary
`period_end` labels the fiscal-year end and `months` is the reporting duration. `amount` retains the displayed value. `unit` distinguishes USD thousand revenue from USD billion volume. Section IDs map each observation and definition to the source extract.
