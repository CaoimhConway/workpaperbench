# Ashtead conditional distribution capacity

This is a hypothetical twelve-month funding analysis using April 30, 2025
facility observations. It is not an estimate of actual dividend capacity or a
legal opinion. All planning cash flows and the incremental borrowing rate are
authored assumptions. The company disclosure supplies the financial covenant
and starting facility observations.

Commitment permits at most **4,750 - 1,351 = 3,399** million of incremental cash
borrowing. Letters of credit are already included in utilisation. Suppressed
availability is collateral-related availability, not another commitment.
The covenant starts applying at **3,616 - 475 = 3,141** million of additional
borrowing. The separate USD 217m difference between the two starting headroom
observations remains unexplained. The scenario does not manufacture a bridge.

## Independent financial model

Let `d` be additional cash borrowing, `x` the special dividend and `e` new cash
equity, all USD million. The annual new-borrowing cash rate is `r = 0.08`.
Existing fixed cash charges are principal 500, interest 500, tax 300 and ordinary
dividends 400, totaling 1,700. Define `H` as EBITDA less net cash capital
expenditure and those fixed charges. It is 1,000, 1,300 and 2,000 in cases a, b
and c. Subtracting each case's working-capital outflow leaves `B = -2,000`.
These intermediate values are reference derivations, not supplied input rows.

The actual economic restrictions are:

- `0 <= d <= 3,399` and `x,e >= 0`.
- Cash above the operating floor is `B + (1-r)d + e - x >= 0`.
- For `d < 3,141`, the financial-performance test is inapplicable.
- For `d >= 3,141`, coverage requires
  `(EBITDA - cash capex) / (1,700 + r*d + x) >= 1`, or `x <= H-r*d`.

Borrowing funds cash uses but also consumes coverage through interest.
Working-capital absorption affects cash without entering this disclosed ratio.
New equity helps cash funding but is neither EBITDA nor a contractual ratio cure.
No actual company forecast, rate-grid forecast or undocumented debt maturity is
being inferred.

## Distribution limits and proof

In the exempt region, cash permits `x <= B+(1-r)d`. Its limit at the trigger is
889.72, approached from below. The trigger itself is outside that exemption.
In the tested region, the upper envelope is the lesser of the increasing cash
line and the decreasing coverage line. Their intersection has `d = H-B`.

| Scenario | Sharp upper limit | Attainable | Economic certificate |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| case_a | 889.72 | No | At the trigger the coverage limit is only 748.72 and falls with further draws. Exempt draws can approach 889.72 arbitrarily closely. |
| case_b | 1,036 | Yes | Draw 3,300. Both cash and coverage bind exactly. |
| case_c | 1,127.08 | Yes | Draw 3,399. Cash and commitment bind. Coverage permits 1,728.08. |

These certificates establish sharpness. Any higher amount violates cash in the
exempt region and cash or coverage in the tested region. No particular witness
is exact-matched by the scorer. Only the amount and attainability flag are
graded.

## Equity support

For a fixed target `x`, active coverage caps draws at `(H-x)/r`. The eligible
draw set is the union of the exempt interval and, when nonempty, the tested
interval capped by commitment and coverage. Because additional borrowing
contributes positive net cash, the least equity is found at the largest allowed
draw or its open limit. Required equity there is `max(0,x-B-(1-r)d)`.

| Scenario | Target 1,000 equity limit | Attainable | Target 1,400 equity limit | Attainable |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| case_a | 110.28 | No | 510.28 | No |
| case_b | 0 | Yes | 510.28 | No |
| case_c | 0 | Yes | 272.92 | Yes |

At an open lower limit, any positive increment to equity makes a nearby lower
draw possible. Exactly the stated equity does not. A zero lower limit is
attainable when an allowed draw already funds the target.

## Validation and limits

The Decimal oracle enumerates branch endpoints and the cash/coverage
intersection. Alternative SQL uses a closed-form upper envelope and admissible
draw intervals. A separate exact-rational two-variable half-space solver checks
cash and covenant inequalities directly, including strict boundary feasibility,
for the original and every control. A separate source/economic review checked
the source PDF pages and identified the non-ABL principal clarification before
freeze. This is not independent human review, which remains pending.

Controls move commitment before the trigger, place the trigger at commitment,
change interest, working capital and earnings, and check irrelevant suppressed
availability/LC composition, monetary scale and row-order invariance. Targeted
wrong submissions apply the covenant always or never, omit borrowing interest,
treat suppressed availability as commitment, subtract letters of credit twice,
declare every limit attained or let cash equity cure EBITDA coverage.

Original claim verdicts are contradicted, supported and unresolved. The two
equivalent evidence paths are Note 18 plus assumptions, or the financial review
plus assumptions. Claims remain tied to the original dossier while SQL is
recomputed on synthetic controls. Optional explanatory prose is not semantically
graded. No empirical difficulty or model performance is established here.
