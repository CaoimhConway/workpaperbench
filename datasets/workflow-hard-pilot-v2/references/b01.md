# Veeco conversion funding and earnings-dependent dilution

This development case constructs a portfolio denominator from conditional instruments. Its closest existing counterparts are workflow-v15/b09, which compares Intuit EPS denominator effects, and c08, which matches Broadcom adjusted measures to diluted share bases. The new decisions are which contractual claims become cash or shares, which potential shares enter EPS despite no economic conversion, which expenses reverse, and how other instruments change a note's earnings inclusion boundary. The v1/b01 cash-investment bridge is a secondary counterpart for the distinction between a financial obligation and its accounting carrying amount.

## EPS portfolio reconstruction

Basic EPS is net income divided by 56.426 million basic weighted shares. The fixed 1.010 million incremental award shares produce a starting diluted denominator of 57.436 million, before notes. Using that denominator for basic EPS would be incorrect.

The issuer's Note 2 treatment provides full potential shares for the 2025 and 2027 notes even when their economic conversion is out of the money. Their numerator adjustments are 1.026 and 1.028 million, respectively. The mandatory-cash 2029 principal creates only excess potential shares, with no note-interest addback in the supplied treatment. This follows the disclosed annual numerator reconciliation as well as the stated principal settlement distinction.

At price 40, the 2029 issue adds 2.120255 million shares and zero earnings. The 2027 incremental earnings per share are `1.028 / 1.78843`, about 0.574806. The 2025 incremental earnings per share are `1.026 / 1.10416755`, about 0.929207. Awards and the 2029 excess are therefore first, then 2027, then 2025. A note enters only when its incremental ratio reduces the current portfolio EPS. FASB's ordering rule is economically consequential because comparisons with the starting EPS alone can include an antidilutive issue.

At income 55 and price 40, the 2025 note appears dilutive by itself. Starting EPS is `55 / 57.436`, while adding only 2025 gives `56.026 / 58.54016755`. But after 2029 and 2027, EPS is `56.028 / 61.344685 = 0.913330959316`, below the 2025 incremental ratio. Adding 2025 now increases EPS. The correct portfolio excludes it. The targeted standalone-EPS mistake exercises this distinction.

Prices 12, 18 and 25 are below the 2029 conversion threshold and consequently have the same EPS sensitivity under these fixed awards and expense assumptions. This does not mean their economic note issuance is identical.

## Reverse thresholds and feasibility

The full-conversion total is 10.76285255 million shares. The two physical-settlement series together issue 2.89259755 million. A target of 5 million therefore lies above those discrete quantities and below total full conversion. All three series are in the money at the solution, and issuance is strictly increasing and continuous there.

`5 = 10.76285255 - 230 / P` gives `P = 39.910790360235748...` USD per share. The result exceeds each contractual conversion threshold. Substitution delivers exactly 5 million gross shares. Below that price the continuous-region issuance is smaller, and earlier regimes cannot reach the target. Above it the issuance is larger. No alternative lower-price regime supplies the target.

For a note with incremental adjustment `A`, shares `S` and ratio `r = A / S`, let `D` be the base-plus-award shares plus all more dilutive potential shares and let `E` be their earnings adjustments. The income neutrality condition is `(N + E) / D = r`, so `N = r × D - E`. The source ordering principle determines the other issues in `D` and `E`. It is not a comparison of the note against basic EPS in isolation.

| Measure | Derivation | Value and unit |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| earnings_2027 | `(1.028 / 1.78843) × (57.436 + 2.120255)` | 34.233282901763 USD million |
| earnings_2025 | `(1.026 / 1.10416755) × (57.436 + 2.120255 + 1.78843) - 1.028` | 55.973898679236 USD million |

Each boundary is neutral at equality, dilutive just above and antidilutive just below. The exact-tie control permits including or excluding its neutral issue. Only the unique minimum EPS is graded, with no exact matching to an arbitrary inclusion witness.

## Executable and independent checks

`scripts/workflow_hard_convertibles.py` provides a Decimal oracle using incremental-EPS ordering. Reference SQL enumerates all eight possible note subsets. A valid alternative uses ordered cumulative windows and evaluates prefix portfolios. Its settlement SQL starts from holder payoff and conserves value between cash and shares, instead of directly classifying the output quantities.

`tests/test_hard_convertibles.py` independently enumerates the EPS subsets with exact rational arithmetic, checks source addback reconciliation, checks holder-value conservation and finds the share-price threshold by bisection. It tests both sides of every earnings threshold across the original inputs and all eight controls. The financial argument rests on the contractual obligation, the issuer's actual numerator reconciliation and the source accounting rule, rather than agreement of two formulas alone.

The controls cover monetary/share scaling, row order, reversed incremental-EPS ranking, prices below every conversion threshold, a carrying-value-only change, a larger 2029 tranche, a changed share target and an exact EPS neutrality tie. Synthetic controls remain separate from the original observations. Changes to reported addback metadata accompany the synthetic expense changes. The larger-tranche control scales face, carrying value and expenses together.

Eight plausible wrong submissions fail numerically: using carrying value for face, paying all principal in cash despite elected physical settlement, full-share EPS for mandatory-cash principal, conditioning EPS on economic maturity conversion, adding back 2029 interest, including all notes regardless of antidilution, testing each series only against initial EPS, and computing standalone earnings boundaries. These failures identify the intended financial distinctions.

Alternative evidence paths are accepted. Annual-report rights and rate observations can replace the redundant 2025 and 2027 indenture extracts. Either the 2029 indenture or the issuer's EPS accounting paragraph independently contradicts the claim that 2029 principal can be settled entirely in shares.
