b02:s01 - PDF pages 4-5, 7 and 10, January 30 token total, asset composition and comparison
At January 30, 2026 at 17:00 Eastern Time, redeemable tokens are 3,608,697,613. Assets are cash USD 129,407,459, repurchase agreements USD 3,118,089,000 and Treasury obligations USD 374,648,750, totaling USD 3,622,145,209. The report is signed February 26, 2026.

b02:s02 - PDF pages 4-5, 7 and 10, February 10 and February 27 token totals, composition and comparison
The February report observes February 10 and February 27, 2026 at 17:00 Eastern Time. These are economic observation dates, while March 24, 2026 is the signature date. Asset values use fair value for repurchase agreements and Treasury holdings.

b02:s03 - PDF page 1 versus pages 3-5, 7 and 9-10, January 16 year labels
The accountant opinion names January 16, 2026, while the management assertion, summary and several financial table headings name January 16, 2025. Repurchase transaction dates include January 16, 2026. The supplied document does not uniquely resolve the conflicting year labels, and no issuer correction is supplied.

b02:s04 - PDF page 5, Exhibit A footnotes, page 7 asset definitions and page 10 Exhibit C notes
All PYUSD tokens are redeemable, with no temporary or permanent nonredeemable categories. The comparison includes timing differences for purchased but unminted and redeemed but unpaid tokens. No such numeric timing differences are reported at these observation times. Stocks of tokens and backing assets do not measure payments flows. Repo holdings and Treasury holdings are separate asset categories, and repo collateral must not be counted again.
