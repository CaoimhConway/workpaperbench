b03:s01 - PDF page 5, total revenue table, all seven period columns and custodial fee footnote
Revenue amounts are USD million and may not sum precisely due to rounding. Since Q1 2025, custodial fee revenue is included in other subscription and services, and prior periods have been recast to this presentation. Corporate interest is additional to net revenue.

b03:s02 - PDF pages 5 and 6, transaction revenue discussion
Q4 2025 consumer spot volume is USD 56 billion and institutional spot volume USD 215 billion. Institutional transaction revenue also reflects derivatives, including Deribit. Consumer revenue is affected by simple/advanced mix and subscriber trading. Revenue divided by spot volume is an apparent ratio, not an identified commission schedule.

b03:s03 - PDF pages 7 and 8, USDC balances, revenue and definitions
The Q4 USDC table uses rounded USD billion average balances and USD million stablecoin revenue. On-platform average includes corporate balances and eligible customer products. Off-platform balances contribute revenue too. These flows and average balances do not measure payments volume or uniquely identify customer reward expense.
