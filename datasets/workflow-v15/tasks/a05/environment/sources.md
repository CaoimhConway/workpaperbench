a05:s02 - PDF page 4, consolidated balance sheets, deferred membership fees
Deferred membership fees are liability stocks at August 31, 2025 and September 1, 2024. Earned membership fees are period revenue. Adding earned fees to the liability change is an arithmetic accrual bridge, but these statements do not separately quantify all membership cash collections, refunds and currency effects.
