# Circle January 2025 report dictionary
USDC in circulation equals total USDC supply on approved blockchains less tokens allowed but not issued and access-denied tokens.

Fair value of assets held in the USDC reserve is the total balance of US-dollar-denominated assets in the Circle Reserve Fund and segregated accounts held by Circle with regulated financial institutions on behalf of USDC holders.

The two observations are point-in-time report dates, not daily balances or transfer flows.

The table contains report dates January 6 and January 31, 2025 at 23:59 UTC. USDC quantities are tokens and reserves are USD fair value. The independent accountants opinion and management signature are dated February 27, 2025. First historical web publication is unknown. Capture retrieval is a separate 2026 date, not contemporaneous availability.

Source: https://6778953.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/6778953/USDCAttestationReports/2025/2025-USDC_Examination-Report-January-25.pdf


One million USDC is 1000000 tokens. USD reserve headroom compares assets with circulation at the report's issuer par redemption convention of USD 1 per USDC. This is not a supplied market price, USD market capitalization or period transfer volume. Daily or dated stock observations are not summed to obtain flow. The changed control is synthetic.
