b06:s01 - Gross Bookings, Revenue and Segment Adjusted EBITDA, Q4 comparison tables
Mobility, Delivery and Freight are mutually exclusive segment rows. Corporate G&A and Platform R&D is a signed deduction outside the segment sum. Adjusted EBITDA margin in the quarterly highlights uses Gross Bookings as denominator. Revenue divided by bookings is a descriptive accounting ratio with heterogeneous segment scope, not a uniquely identified commission.

b06:s02 - Definitions of Gross Bookings, Trips and Monthly Active Platform Consumers
Gross Bookings includes applicable taxes, tolls and fees and excludes tips. Trips comprises Mobility rides and Delivery orders. MAPCs is the monthly unique Mobility/Delivery consumer count averaged over the quarter, counting a consumer once despite multiple product use. It is not a count of all distinct consumers across the entire quarter.

b06:s03 - Quarterly platform highlights and non-GAAP definitions
Trips per MAPC is presented on a monthly basis. Segment Adjusted EBITDA has segment revenue less direct costs and applicable exclusions. The total includes corporate costs. A change in a revenue/bookings ratio does not separately identify changes in contractual commission, principal-versus-agent presentation, mix or incentives.
