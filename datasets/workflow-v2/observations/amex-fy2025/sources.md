a10:s01 - Earnings Tables printed page 1, consolidated statements of income, seven period columns
Total revenue is net of interest expense. Discount revenue, net card fees, service/other revenue and net interest income sum to total revenue. Provision and expense amounts subtract before pretax income, then tax subtracts to net income. Preserve quarters and annual totals and their rounding residuals.

a10:s02 - Earnings Tables printed pages 4 and 14, network volumes and Appendix II footnote G
Billed business is transaction volume on American Express-issued payment products including cash advances. Network volume also includes processed partner-card and alternative-payment volumes. The FX convention applies current-period exchange rates to the prior-year amounts, rather than restating current revenue. Card counts include supplemental/partner scope, and neither network nor billed volume uniquely identifies a causal discount-fee price effect.

a10:s03 - FY/Q4 earnings release page 1, matching FX-adjusted prior columns
The release prints prior FX-adjusted billed business of USD 412.7 billion for Q4 and USD 1,555.5 billion for the year. Prior FX-adjusted revenue is USD 17,349 million and USD 66,083 million. Current reported amounts remain the numerator. Prior adjusted EPS removes an Accertify transaction gain and differs from reported EPS, so adjusted EPS growth is not reported net-income growth.
