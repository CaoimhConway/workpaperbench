b04:s01 - Fourth-quarter/full-year release, consolidated operations tables and adjusted-expense reconciliation
Quarter comparisons are December 2024, December 2025 and September 2025, annual comparisons are 2024 and 2025. Adjusted expenses subtract stock compensation, significant legal/tax settlements and provision for credit losses. A negative settlement expense is a benefit, so removing it raises adjusted expense. The supplied 2024 reconciliation does not exclude its credit-loss provision, while the 2025 reconciliation does. The actual provisions are separately retained and are nonzero in both years.

b04:s02 - Additional Q4 operating data and preliminary January 2026 operating data
Q4 crypto notional volume comprises USD 34 billion on the Robinhood app and USD 48 billion at Bitstamp, totaling USD 82 billion. January preliminary volumes are USD 8.7 billion on the app and USD 14.2 billion at Bitstamp, totaling USD 22.9 billion. Quarter and month volumes have different durations.

b04:s03 - Key performance definitions, Funded Customers, Total Platform Assets and Net Deposits
Bitstamp closed in June 2025 and its customers, crypto assets and net deposits entered the disclosed metrics then. TradePMR client net deposits are excluded because of data limitations, though its managed assets enter Total Platform Assets. Total platform asset changes cannot be identified as organic customer flows or investment returns from the supplied revenue and notional-volume tables.
