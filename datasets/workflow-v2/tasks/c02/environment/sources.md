c02:s01 - PDF page 7, Non-GAAP Results, operating-income reconciliation, all five period columns
Reported operating income is reconciled to non-GAAP operating income through stock-based and deferred compensation, amortization, acquisition-related expense, loss-contingency reversal and lease-related impairment. Reconciliation amounts are USD million.

c02:s02 - PDF page 2, Financial Targets and FY2025 Financial Highlights
FY2025 ending Total Adobe ARR was USD 25.20 billion. Revaluation raised the opening FY2026 basis to USD 25.66 billion, a USD 460 million increase primarily attributed to currency-rate changes. The FY2026 ending ARR growth target is 10.2%. Revenue guidance is USD 25.90 to 26.10 billion. Targets exclude Semrush contribution.

c02:s03 - PDF page 2, Disclosure Updates, and page 9, non-GAAP information
FY2026 reporting and guidance focus on customer-group subscription revenue and total-company ARR growth. Digital Media and Digital Experience subscription revenue remain supplemental. ARR is a year-end run-rate measure, and revenue is a period measure. Non-GAAP results are supplemental to GAAP.
