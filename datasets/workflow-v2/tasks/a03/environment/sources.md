The prior adjusted-expense definition removes stock compensation, depreciation, digital-asset gains/losses and Foundation share contributions. The new definition also removes payroll tax on stock compensation, legal expenses, acquisition costs and annual restructuring. Both definitions are supplied for comparison, with signed reconciliation adjustments.

a03:s02 - PDF page 10, consolidated statements of operations, and pages 14 and 15, adjusted EBITDA
Quarter columns are the three months ended December 31, 2025 and 2024, annual columns are years ended those dates. Reserve income and other revenue are separate. RLDC is total revenue and reserve income less total distribution, transaction and other costs.

a03:s03 - PDF pages 2 and 3, operating indicators and measure footnotes
RLDC margin divides RLDC by total revenue and reserve income. Adjusted EBITDA margin divides adjusted EBITDA by RLDC. Reserve yield changes and average circulating USDC both affect reserve income, so the supplied expense and income statements do not identify exact constant-balance interest-rate attribution.
