a09:s01 - PDF printed pages 4 and 21, segment summary and pretax-to-segment reconciliation
Intersegment fees reduce consolidated revenue through eliminations. Segment operating income is not GAAP pretax income. Signed add-backs include corporate expenses, India joint-venture losses, restructuring, other expense, interest and purchase-accounting amortization. Q4 and full-year columns have different scope.

a09:s02 - PDF printed page 5, Star India transaction, and pages 7 and 17, subscriptions and definitions
Star India consolidated through November 14, 2024. The subsequent India joint venture is 37 percent owned and recorded in equity income. Disney+ Hotstar is absent from current Q4 DTC comparisons. Disney+ and Hulu subscription counts include bundle subscriptions in both services, so summing them counts service subscriptions rather than unique people or households. The supplied counts compare Q4 with Q3, not prior-year Q4.

a09:s03 - PDF printed pages 6 and 7, direct-to-consumer operating discussion
DTC growth includes prices, subscriber changes, content and marketing costs and the Star India transaction. These observations do not uniquely quantify an organic constant-scope revenue-growth rate or isolated pricing effects. Subscription stock changes are not quarter revenue flows.
