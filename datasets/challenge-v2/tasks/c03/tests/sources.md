# Block FY2024 and FY2023 annual disclosures

c03:s01 - Revenue by source (USD thousand)
2024: transaction-based 6,613,680, subscription and services-based 7,164,799, hardware 143,369, Bitcoin 10,199,205, total net revenue 24,121,053.
2023: transaction-based 6,315,301, subscription and services-based 5,944,842, hardware 157,178, Bitcoin 9,498,302, total net revenue 21,915,623.

c03:s02 - Cost of revenue by source (USD thousand)
Bitcoin costs were 9,910,386 in 2024 and 9,293,113 in 2023. Total cost of revenue was 15,232,017 and 14,410,737. The filing separately reports transaction-based, subscription and services-based, hardware, Bitcoin and acquired-technology amortization costs.

c03:s03 - Bitcoin revenue definition
Bitcoin revenue is the total sale amount of bitcoin sold to customers. It varies with both customer demand and bitcoin market price. The filing presents Bitcoin costs separately from Bitcoin revenue.

c03:s04 - Cash App comparisons
Bitcoin represented 63% and 65% of Cash App revenue in 2024 and 2023, and 6% and 5% of Cash App gross profit. These percentages are scoped to Cash App and are not consolidated percentages.

c03:s05 - Period and unit convention
All revenue and cost amounts are annual flows for the years ended December 31 and are reported in USD thousands. `Bitcoin gross profit` in this analysis means Bitcoin revenue less the separately reported Bitcoin costs. It is a derived analytical amount. It is not equal to Bitcoin revenue, and it is not separately captioned as a line in the filing.

## Data dictionary
`period_end` identifies the annual period end, `months` gives duration, and `amount` retains the displayed number. `unit` is USD thousand except the Cash App shares, which are percent. The source group uses the FY2024 original filing's own FY2023 comparator and financial categories.
