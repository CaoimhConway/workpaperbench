b05:s01 - PDF pages 10 and 11, net revenue classification and operating-data table
All five columns are three-month periods. Accounts are active at period end, while payment transactions and TPV are quarter flows. Revenue includes transaction revenue and other value-added services. Rounded amounts can produce small arithmetic differences.

b05:s02 - PDF page 11, operating-data footnotes 1 to 4
Active accounts have completed a transaction in the previous twelve months. One user can have multiple accounts. Reported transactions per active account divides trailing-twelve-month transactions by end-of-period active accounts. Transactions and TPV exclude gateway-only activity and net payment reversals.

b05:s03 - PDF pages 7, 12 and 13, income statement and transaction-margin reconciliation
Transaction margin dollars equals net revenue less transaction expense and transaction/credit losses. The measure excluding customer-balance interest subtracts that interest. Customer interest is within other value-added services. Neither margin dollars nor the ratio of revenue to TPV is the value of payments or a uniquely identified processing price.
