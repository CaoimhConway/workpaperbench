b07:s01 - PDF pages 19, 23 and 25, quarterly summary, key metrics and cash reconciliation
Nights and Seats Booked includes stay nights and service/experience seats, net of cancellations and alterations. GBV includes host earnings, service fees, cleaning fees and taxes. Printed GBV is rounded to one decimal billion, so its quotient using rounded unit counts can differ from the separately published ADR.

b07:s02 - PDF pages 10, 17 and 25, implied take rate and non-GAAP definitions
Implied take rate is revenue divided by GBV. Revenue timing follows stays and experiences while bookings enter GBV when booked. It does not identify a contractual fee rate. EBITDA margin uses revenue, and FCF margin uses same-period revenue. TTM margins use the four-quarter sum of both numerator and denominator.

b07:s03 - PDF page 25, full twelve-quarter cash reconciliation and TTM disclosures
Quarter FCF equals operating cash less property purchases. Trailing twelve months is the latest four quarters, not four times the latest quarter. Booking seasonality makes quarterly and TTM liquidity rates different. Customer funds do not enter FCF except interest earned on them.
