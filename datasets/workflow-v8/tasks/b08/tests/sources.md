Subscription and merchant solutions are revenue categories with separate costs. GMV is the value of platform-facilitated orders, net of refunds, including shipping, handling, duties and VAT. The operating reconciliation deducts total operating expenses from the two category gross-profit amounts.

b08:s02 - PDF page 9, MRR and GMV definitions
MRR is all subscription plans in effect on the final day of the period, excluding variable platform fees and assuming maintenance in the following month. The annual and Q4 columns repeat the same December stock. Twelve times exit MRR is a hypothetical run rate, not observed annual subscription revenue or a missing-revenue measure.

FCF equals operating cash less capital expenditure, and FCF margin divides by total revenue. Merchant solutions includes more than payment processing, while GMV is broader than payments volume.
